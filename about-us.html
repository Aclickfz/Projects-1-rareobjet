about JustAclick

Kalista is an indigenously curated label with a contemporary take on refined Indian clothing. Designed for the progressive and innovative woman of today, who is ready to break the monotony and experiment with color and style, our design philosophy is truly eclectic, with a generous use of colors and patterns inspired by the craftsmanship and cultural influences from all over the world.

Striking yet free spirited, our ensembles bring contemporary aesthetics to traditional wear while reinventing silhouettes and constantly pushing the boundaries of affordable luxury.

The label focuses on producing an array of delectable festive wear pieces meticulously handcrafted with impeccable detailing. Paying tribute to the Indian craftsmanship, the label also carries intrinsic elements of Victorian and Persian cultures with an emphasis on fine tailoring and sophisticated cuts incorporating traditional Indian embroideries, embellished in a refined, modern approach.

Our Flagship stores are in Mumbai at KalaGhoda and in Delhi at Shahpurjat and our designs are also retailing through the best multi-designer stores in Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad , Chennai , Bangalore and Kolkata in India as well as prominent stores in London, Dubai and Jakarta.
